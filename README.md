# 👋 Hi, I'm Timothy!

I'm a passionate undergraduate student at James Madison University studying and performing mathematical research in algebraic geometry, specifically working on topics in Chow theory regarding the intersections of algebraic curves. In the spring of 2026, for the Hilary term, I will be an Associate Member at Oxford University, taking graduate coursework in algebraic geometry. This term, I am also taking graduate differential topology at the University of Virginia with Thomas Koberda alongside the Weil Conjectures and Etale Cohomology Seminar at Princeton (courtesy of Dhruv Goel). I am especially interested in moduli problems of curves, and topics related to the Chow ring of moduli spaces of curves. Additionally, I participated in the 2026 summer REU at JMU on homeomorphism-induced fixed-points of character varieties of 3-manifolds and their associated Chern-Simons invariants. Over the past few years, my work has been split across pure and applied math, however, I have happily settled in pure mathematics at this point. 

My grants at the moment are:
 - The JMU summer REU ($7800 via the Elizabeth McConnell Bliss and Droms Endowments) at JMU researching representation varieties with Dr. David Duncan.
 - The Brakke-Mason Research Grant ($1900) to research the Bruhat Order on the set of fixed point free involutions with Dr. Rebecca Field and Ilani Axelrod-Freed at MIT.
